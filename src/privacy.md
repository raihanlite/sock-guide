# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

Sock Guide is a small, independent reading site. This policy explains what data is collected when you read it.

## What we collect
We collect no personal information directly. There are no accounts, no comments and no contact form that stores your details on this site.

## Cookies and advertising
There are currently no advertisements running on this site. The ad slots in the page layout are empty: they contain no advertising code, load no advertising script, and set no advertising cookies.

If display advertising is enabled in future (for example through Google AdSense), the advertising provider may use cookies to show ads and measure them. This policy will be updated before any advertising goes live, and will name the provider and link to its own privacy policy.

## Third-party services
- **Fonts:** pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches a font, Google may see your IP address and browser details, as it does for any website that loads Google Fonts.
- **Links out:** guides cite published sources and link to them. Those sites have their own privacy policies; this policy does not cover them.

## Children's privacy
Sock Guide is written for adults buying socks for work and sport. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy
If how this site handles data changes — for example, if advertising or analytics are added — this page will be updated and the date above will change.
