# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sock Guide collects when you visit the site, how it is used, and the choices you have. Sock Guide is a buying-guide website; reading it requires no account and no sign-up.

## Information we collect

Information you send us. If you contact us by email, or ask to join the newsletter, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use it only to reply to you, or to send the newsletter you asked for.

Usage information. Like most websites, Sock Guide may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which guides are read and how visitors arrive. That information is aggregated and does not identify individual readers.

## Cookies

Sock Guide itself sets no tracking cookies. Some third-party services used by the site may set cookies — see the next section.

## Advertising and third-party services

Sock Guide is supported by advertising. When advertising is enabled on this site, it is served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads), and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address in order to serve the font files.

## Links to other websites

Guides link out to sources, including Wikimedia Commons for photo credits. Sock Guide is not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites.

## Your choices

You can ask at any time what personal information we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, through the [Contact page](/contact/). Every newsletter email includes a way to unsubscribe.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
