Sock Guide is a small site of sock buying guides — one guide for each job, sport or kind of weather, with clear picks, a comparison table and answers to common questions.

Sock Guide is a sock information blog, not a shop and not an affiliate site. Topics come from readers' real questions about socks. Facts are checked against cited sources — guides, maker specifications and research — and each article lists its sources. No sock is tested hands-on here, and no testing is claimed. Nothing is sold here and there are no affiliate links or commissions. If something is wrong or out of date, reader corrections are welcome (Contact page).
