## Short answer
There is no single best work-boot sock — the right pair depends on the job. A construction worker standing on concrete needs different cushioning from a warehouse worker in a hot building. This guide picks one sock per job, using the 2026 guides from BootsGuru, Product Rankers and The Foot Facts.

## How to choose socks for work boots
- **Match cushion to the floor:** concrete = maximum cushion; warehouse = light cushion and breathability.
- **Match material to sweat:** merino for odour and warmth, synthetics for fast drying in heat.
- **Match height to the boot:** over-the-calf for tall boots, crew for short boots.
- **Steel toes need toe-box cushion** — the cap presses on the same spot all day.

## FAQ
**Why do my feet hurt in work boots?** Usually the sock, not the boot: cotton socks stay wet, thin socks let the steel cap press, and socks that slide down bunch under the heel.
**Should work boot socks be thick or thin?** Thick for concrete and cold; thin and breathable for hot warehouses. Never so thick the boot fits tight — that cuts circulation.
**How often should I replace work socks?** When the heel or toe thins, the elastic gives out, or the cushion flattens — typically after 6–12 months of daily wear.
