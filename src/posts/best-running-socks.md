## FAQ
<details class="faq"><summary>What type of socks should I wear for running?</summary><div class="a">Moisture-wicking fibres (a merino blend or a synthetic), a snug fit with no spare fabric, a seamless toe, and a height that clears the shoe collar. Avoid cotton: it holds sweat against the skin and raises friction and blister risk.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>Is it better to wear thin or thick socks for running?</summary><div class="a">Thin for racing, hot weather and a close shoe fit; medium cushion for daily training and long runs. Thick only if you need the padding and your shoes have room for it — a thick sock in a snug shoe squeezes the foot and can blister faster than a fitted thin one. Fit matters more than thickness either way.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>Do you really need special socks for running?</summary><div class="a">For regular running, yes: a running sock manages moisture and friction in a way ordinary socks do not. But it does not have to be the priciest pair — any fitted, moisture-wicking athletic sock beats a cotton everyday sock.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>Do I need cushioned or no cushion running socks?</summary><div class="a">Light or no cushion for racing and hot weather; medium cushion for daily miles and long runs. Heavy cushion is rarely needed for road running. And no — cushioned socks do not measurably slow most runners; fit, moisture-wicking fibre, a seamless toe and padding in the right places matter more.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>Are toe socks better for blisters?</summary><div class="a">For blisters between the toes, yes: the five-toe design stops skin-on-skin friction, which is what causes them. That is the Injinji case. If toe socks are not for you, a double-layer or liner-plus-outer system works on the same principle — the friction happens between the layers instead of on your skin.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>Do cushioned running socks make you run slower?</summary><div class="a">Short answer: no, not measurably for most runners. Fit (no bunching), a moisture-wicking fibre, a seamless toe and strategic padding protect the foot without a meaningful loss of ground feel.</div></details>
