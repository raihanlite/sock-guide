There is no separate sock technology for men. The fibres are the same, the knits are the same, and a good merino crew does not know or care who is wearing it. What differs is sizing — men's socks are sold by men's shoe size, and the size runs go further — and use: most men need a small rotation that covers the office, the work boot, the run and the cold day, rather than one perfect pair. So this guide picks by use, not by marketing. Six socks, one for each job a drawer has to do. The women's version of this guide — same by-use approach, women's sizing — is [Best Socks for Women](/posts/best-socks-for-women/). If you want the background first — heights, fits and what each type is for — start with [Types of Socks](/posts/types-of-socks/) and [How to Choose the Right Socks](/posts/how-to-choose-the-right-socks/).

The short version: a cotton-blend half calf for everyday, a cheap cushioned crew pack for work, a merino crew for the days that run long, a thin dress sock for suits, a running-specific no-show for miles, and a thick merino crew for cold weather. If your feet sweat a lot, the rotation changes — the sweaty-feet picks are in [Best Socks for Sweaty Feet](/posts/best-socks-for-sweaty-feet/), and the fibre-by-fibre case for each material is in [Sock Fabrics](/posts/sock-fabrics/).

## FAQ
<details class="faq"><summary>What socks should men wear every day?</summary><div class="a">A midweight crew or half calf in a cotton blend or merino, matched to the shoe and the day: a cotton blend for the office and dry days, merino if your feet sweat or the day runs long. Thin, smooth dress socks belong with dress shoes; thick hiking socks belong in boots, not in slim trainers. The step-by-step choice is in <a href="/posts/how-to-choose-the-right-socks/">How to Choose the Right Socks</a>.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>How many pairs of socks should a man own?</summary><div class="a">At least one pair for each day you wear shoes, plus spares — a week's worth is a practical minimum, and more if you exercise daily or work long shifts. On top of that, keep a few dress pairs for suits and a few sport pairs for running or the gym, so the everyday pairs are not doing every job. Rotating pairs also makes each pair last longer, because the knit recovers its shape between wears.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>What socks go with dress shoes?</summary><div class="a">Thin, smooth dress socks in an over-the-calf or extended crew height, so no bare leg shows when you sit. Match the socks to your trousers, not your shoes — dark navy or charcoal with a navy suit, black with black trousers — and keep the fabric fine enough not to bulk out a slim shoe. The Mack Weldon pick above is built for exactly this; the height rules are in <a href="/posts/types-of-socks/">Types of Socks</a>.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>Are ankle socks or crew socks better for men?</summary><div class="a">Neither is better in general — it depends on the shoe and the day. Crew socks suit boots, trousers and cold weather; ankle and no-show socks suit trainers, shorts and the gym. The mistake is wearing one height for everything: a crew sock bunched into a low trainer, or an ankle sock under trousers that shows bare leg. The full comparison is in <a href="/posts/crew-socks-vs-ankle-socks/">Crew Socks vs Ankle Socks</a>.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>Do men need different socks for running?</summary><div class="a">Yes — running socks are built to manage sweat, hold their fit and prevent blisters, which everyday cotton crews do not do: cotton stays wet, bunches and rubs over miles. A running-specific sock (the Darn Tough pick above, or any of the six in <a href="/posts/best-running-socks/">Best Running Socks</a>) is the cheapest upgrade in a runner's kit.</div></details>

<details class="faq"><summary>What size socks do men wear?</summary><div class="a">Men's socks are sold by US men's shoe size, and every maker prints its own size chart — check it before ordering, because the ranges differ. Darn Tough runs S to XXL, Bombas runs M to XL, and Mack Weldon's Everyday line comes in one size only. If you sit between sizes, size up: Amazon's own summary flags the Darn Tough Standard Crew as running small.</div></details>
