## Short answer
For most workers, the **Darn Tough Steely Boot Midweight** is the best all-round work sock: a merino-wool blend, boot height, reinforced heel and toe, and a lifetime guarantee. If you buy in bulk or want the lowest cost per pair, **Dickies Dri-Tech** multipacks are the value pick. For maximum cushion on concrete, **Thorlos 12-Hour Shift** is the specialist.

## How to choose work socks
- **Material:** merino wool wicks sweat and resists odour but costs more; synthetics (polyester/nylon) dry fast and cost less; cotton holds sweat — avoid it for long, sweaty shifts.
- **Cushion:** full cushion for concrete and steel toes; light cushion for hot warehouses.
- **Height:** crew or over-the-calf so the sock stays up inside tall boots.
- **Reinforcement:** reinforced heel and toe — the first places work socks wear out.
- **Fit:** a performance fit that does not bunch inside the boot matters more than thickness.

## FAQ
**Are cotton work socks OK?** For short, cool shifts, yes. For long, sweaty shifts, no — cotton absorbs sweat and holds it against the skin, which causes blisters.
**How many pairs do I need?** A 6-pack covers a work week if you wash at the weekend; merino pairs can be worn twice between washes, synthetics usually not.
**Do I need over-the-calf socks?** For 8–10 inch boots, yes — crew socks can slide down and bunch. For short boots, crew height is fine.
