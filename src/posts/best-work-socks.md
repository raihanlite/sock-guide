## FAQ

**Are merino work socks worth the extra money?** For most boot wearers, yes — merino manages sweat and odour better across long shifts, and socks like the Darn Tough Steely carry a lifetime replacement guarantee, which changes the maths. If your boots live in hot conditions and you wash socks daily, a fast-drying synthetic like the Carhartt Force is the smarter spend.

**How many pairs of work socks should I own?** At least one pair per working day, plus a spare. Rotating pairs lets the knit recover between wears and roughly doubles how long each pair lasts compared with wearing the same pair back-to-back. [How to Choose the Right Socks](/posts/how-to-choose-the-right-socks/) covers rotation, fit checks and when to replace a pair in full.

**Why do my socks wear out at the heel first?** Heel strike plus boot friction. A cushioned heel delays it, correct boot fit helps more, and trimming toenails and keeping feet dry reduces abrasion inside the sock. If one boot always kills the same spot, check that boot's lining for a rough seam.

**Can I wear cotton socks for work?** You can, but in boots it is the most common cause of blisters and cold, damp feet. Cotton holds several times its weight in water and loses insulation when wet. Any blend on this page will outperform it on a long shift. The full fibre-by-fibre comparison is in [Cotton vs Merino Wool Socks](/posts/cotton-vs-merino-wool-socks/).
