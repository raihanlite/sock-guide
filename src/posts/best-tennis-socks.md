## How to choose tennis socks
- **Cushion:** thick cushion absorbs shock on hard courts; light cushion stays cooler and fits tighter shoes.
- **Blister protection:** look for seamless toes, moisture-wicking fibres (Thor-Lon, CoolMax) and a snug, anatomical fit.
- **Height:** crew is the classic tennis height; no-show works for casual play but can slip.
- **Compression:** light arch compression helps support during lateral movement; full compression socks (e.g. OS1st) suit players with plantar fasciitis.

## FAQ
**Crew or no-show for tennis?** Crew is the safer choice — it protects the ankle from shoe rub and stays put during lateral movement. No-show is fine for casual hitting.
**Do tennis socks really prevent blisters?** Cushioning and moisture-wicking help a lot, but fit matters more: a sock that slides causes blisters whatever it is made of.
**How many pairs do I need?** Two to three pairs per week of play, rotated and washed cold.
